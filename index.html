Chloe Yang 杨珺轲

I'm a first year Ph.D student in Biostatistics in The Ohio State Unisveristy. I earned my bachelor's degree in Statistics with minors in Computer Science and Mathematics from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

In my undergraduate years, I was fortunate to work with Prof. Pamela Martinez, Prof. Kelly Findley, and Dr. Sophie Larsen.

My research develops statistical models and machine learning methods for biological and health applications, with particular focus on infectious disease epidemiology.
