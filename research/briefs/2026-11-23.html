New this week

LLM Judges

AltRA-Test: An Alternative Annotator Test for LLM Judges Without Shared Annotators

Method: A bootstrap non-inferiority test on inter-annotator agreement that swaps one human label per item for an LLM label, proven to hold asymptotic level alpha.

Why it matters: Lifts the earlier Alternative Annotator Test's requirement that every item share the same human annotators, which held back its use on datasets labeled by different annotator subsets.

Research acceleration: The jagged frontier
