Methods Matrix

Which statistical method answers which agent-evaluation question. Start from the problem you have, then read across.

ProblemMethodUse whenWatch out forRead
How precise is this score? CLT standard error, 95% CI Always; roughly 30+ independent tasks Too narrow when tasks are clustered Miller 2024
Tasks share a source (repo, document, website) Cluster-robust SE; random-effects model Several tasks come from the same unit Few clusters make cluster SEs unstable Miller 2024
Is agent A better than agent B? Paired difference (t-test, permutation, or bootstrap) Both agents ran on the same tasks Testing many agent pairs without correcting for multiple comparisons Dror et al. 2018
Ranking many agents from head-to-head votes Bradley–Terry model with bootstrap CIs Only pairwise preferences are available Rankings shift with which pairs were sampled Chiang et al. 2024
How big must the benchmark be? Power analysis / sample-size formula Before building or running an eval Guessing the variance too optimistically Card et al. 2020
Can it succeed if allowed retries? Unbiased pass@k estimator n ≥ k trials per task Rewards luck; hides inconsistency Chen et al. 2021
Does it succeed every time? pass^k Reliability matters (customer-facing agents) Needs many trials per task to estimate well Yao et al. 2024
How much of the variance is the task vs. the run? Hierarchical model (trials nested in tasks), ICC Repeated trials per task Binary outcomes need a GLMM, not a linear model Madaan et al. 2024
An LLM judge scores the outputs Agreement vs. a human audit set (kappa, confusion matrix) Any judge-scored metric Raw percent agreement overstates quality when classes are imbalanced Thakur et al. 2024
Correcting a judge's bias in the headline score Misclassification correction (Rogan–Gladen); prediction-powered inference You have a small human-labeled subset Judge error may differ between agents Rogan & Gladen 1978, Boyeau et al. 2024
Judge prefers longer answers Regression adjustment for a confounder A known nuisance variable drives scores Adjusting for something that is actually part of quality Dubois et al. 2024
Items differ in difficulty and informativeness Item response theory Building, pruning, or shrinking a benchmark Needs many agents to fit item parameters Rodriguez et al. 2021, Polo et al. 2024
Better accuracy at what price? Cost–accuracy Pareto frontier Comparing agents with different compute budgets Token prices change over time Kapoor et al. 2024
