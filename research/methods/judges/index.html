LLM Judges

An LLM judge is a measurement instrument with its own error. Agreement, bias, and correction.

Key idea

This is the measurement-error problem from epidemiology. A judge's labels are like an imperfect diagnostic test with its own sensitivity and specificity. A small human-labeled audit set lets you estimate and correct the judge's bias; prediction-powered inference turns that correction into valid confidence intervals.
