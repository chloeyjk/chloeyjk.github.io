Benchmark Validity

Does the benchmark measure what we think it does? Construct validity, item difficulty, and benchmark design.

Key idea

Items differ in difficulty and in how well they separate strong agents from weak ones. Item response theory models both, the same way it is used for psychometric tests, which helps build smaller, more informative benchmarks and explains why the same score can mean different things on different item mixes.
