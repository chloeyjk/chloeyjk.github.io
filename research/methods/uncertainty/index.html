Uncertainty & Error Bars

How much of a reported score is signal? Standard errors, clustered designs, and power.

Key idea

Treat the eval set as a sample of n tasks from a larger population. The mean score has standard error s/√n, where s is the standard deviation of per-task scores. When tasks come in groups (several questions about the same document or repository), per-task scores are correlated and a cluster-robust standard error is needed, usually a wider one.
